How Watch together works

Instead of streaming compressed video over a laggy screen share, everyone in a Watch together room plays their own local copy of the episode at full quality and with their own subtitles and audio track.

Anibeam connects everyone through a lightweight room server so play, pause, seeking, and playback speed stay in sync across up to 10 people.

Start a room and invite friends

You can start a room while already watching an episode, or open an empty room first and pick the episode together:

  1. Click Watch together in the top-right corner of the player (or press Ctrl+J or click Join room at the bottom of the sidebar from anywhere in the app).
  2. Click Start a room. If it is your first time, type the display name you want friends to see and click Start the room.
  3. Share the room with your friends using whichever way is easiest:
    • Click Copy link to send a clickable rooms.anibeam.app link.
    • Read or send them the 6-character Room code.
    • Let friends scan the QR code displayed right in the room panel.
    • If Discord status is enabled, friends can also join via Discord (switch between Invite only and Open in the room panel).
Wait for everyone to get ready

As the host, Anibeam shows who has the episode open and ready. You can click Wait for everyone so playback starts automatically once everyone is ready, or Start anyway whenever you want to begin.

Join a friend's room

You can jump into a room in three ways:

  • Click the link: Open the rooms.anibeam.app link your friend sent and click Open in Anibeam.
  • Enter the code in Anibeam: Click Join room in the sidebar (or press Ctrl+J), type the 6-letter code (or paste the full link), and click Join. Uppercase and lowercase both work.
  • From the web: Visit rooms.anibeam.app and type the code there to open the room's join page.

If the room's episode is already in your library, click Open the episode in the room panel and Anibeam jumps straight into playback synced with the host.

What if you don't have the episode yet?

If the host picks an episode that isn't in your library, your name shows Doesn't have it with a Get media button right next to it:

  1. Click Get media. If the show has multiple episodes, Anibeam asks whether you want This episode or the Whole show.
  2. Anibeam asks the host's app which release they are watching and downloads the matching episode straight into your library.
  3. Everyone in the room sees a clean progress bar and time remaining under your name. As soon as it finishes, you're ready to watch in sync!
Requires downloads set up

Fetching episodes in a room uses your own torrent client and download sources. Make sure Downloads are working on your computer first.

Fix slight timing differences between copies

If you and the host downloaded different video releases of the same episode, one file might have an extra second or two of studio logo or black screen at the start. You don't need to re-download the same file to stay in step:

  • Use the automatic suggestion: When Anibeam detects a difference in where the opening theme begins, the room panel shows a line like The openings suggest 1.5 s. Click Use it to align your copy immediately.
  • Nudge it by ear: Under Your offset in the room panel, click the - (Half a second earlier) or + (Half a second later) buttons until the dialogue lines up with your friends.

Leave a room and room settings

  • Leave: Click Leave the room at the bottom of the room panel at any time.
  • Change your display name: Click Change next to your name in the room panel before joining, or edit Your name in watch together rooms under SettingsPlayback.
  • Room server (advanced): Under SettingsPlayback > Advanced, Room server points to Anibeam's public relay by default. Leave this untouched unless you host your own room server.
