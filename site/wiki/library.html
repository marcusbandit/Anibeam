Add your anime folder

Anibeam builds your library by reading folders that already live on your computer or external drives.

  1. Open SettingsLibrary.
  2. Click Add folder.
  3. Select the parent folder that contains your show folders (for example, ~/Videos/Anime or D:\Anime).

Scanning starts right away, and posters fill in on Library and Home as Anibeam identifies each show. If your library is currently empty, you can also click Add a folder directly on the empty Library screen.

Common mistake

Pick the folder above your shows, not an individual show's folder. Adding Anime/Shell City directly makes Anibeam look for show folders inside Shell City instead of treating it as one series.

You can add as many folders as you like, across different drives. Each subfolder inside them becomes a show in your library.

Name folders and files

You don't need a rigid renaming tool for Anibeam to understand your collection. Standard anime file names work out of the box as long as you follow a few simple habits:

  • One folder per show: Name the folder after the show's title (English or romaji both work well).
  • Keep episode numbers in file names: Common styles like Shell City - 01.mkv, Shell City S01E01.mkv, and bracketed release names like [Group] Shell City - 01 [1080p].mkv all parse cleanly.
  • Later seasons: Put Season 2 either in its own folder (Shell City Season 2/) or inside a Season 2/ subfolder within the main show folder.
  • Movies: Put standalone anime films inside a folder named Movies, one video file per movie.
  • External subtitles: Keep .ass or .srt files in the same folder with the same base name as the episode and Anibeam loads them automatically.
Anime/
  Shell City/
    Shell City - 01.mkv
    Shell City - 01.ass          # external subtitle picked up automatically
    Shell City - 02.mkv
    Season 2/
      Shell City S02E01.mkv
  Inkbound/
    [Group] Inkbound - 01 [1080p].mkv
  Movies/
    Two Skies.mkv

Openings, endings, trailers, and specials (such as files tagged NCOP, NCED, or OVA) are automatically separated under Openings, endings and more on the show's page so they don't clutter your episode list.

How scanning works

When you add a folder, Anibeam runs through four quick steps in the background:

  1. Finds video files: Walks your folder and pairs episode numbers, seasons, specials, and external subtitle files.
  2. Identifies each show: Looks up folder names on AniList first, then falls back to TVmaze for shows that aren't anime. You can change that lookup order under SettingsLibrary.
  3. Fetches artwork and details: Downloads posters, banners, episode titles, air dates, IMDb scores, and related seasons in the same franchise.
  4. Syncs your progress: If AniList or MyAnimeList is connected, pulls in what you have already watched so cards show accurate progress immediately.
Your files stay untouched

Anibeam only reads your library folders. It never renames, moves, or deletes any of your files unless you explicitly ask it to (for example with Delete watched or Rolling downloads, which move files to your system trash).

After the first scan, Anibeam watches your folders automatically, so new episodes appear as soon as they finish downloading or get copied in. If you ever want to trigger a manual check, click Rescan next to a folder in SettingsLibrary, or click Scan all.

Browse, group, and hide shows

Once your library is scanned, the Library page gives you several ways to explore it:

  • Top tabs: Switch between All, Series, Movies, Watched, Hidden, On my list (your Watching and Planning shows from AniList), Stats (watch time, top genres, and a shareable year recap), and Fix matches.
  • Franchises together: Turn on Franchises together in the Library toolbar to group sequels, prequels, and spin-offs under one franchise heading. Each show's page also has a Related section showing seasons you own and ones you are missing.
  • Act on multiple shows at once: Hold Ctrl and click cards (or Shift+click a range, or Ctrl+A) to rate, move to a list, hide, rescan, or match several shows in one go.
  • Hide a show: Open a show and click Hide to remove it from Home, Library, and recommendations. Hiding a show also pauses AniList and MyAnimeList syncing for it until you unhide it. To view hidden shows again, turn on Show hidden shows in SettingsLibrary.
  • Airing calendar file: Turn on Airing times in a calendar in SettingsLibrary to generate an anibeam.ics calendar file you can subscribe to in Apple Calendar, Thunderbird, or other calendar apps.

Fix a show that wasn't recognized (or matched wrong)

If a folder has an unusual name, extra release tags, or a typo, Anibeam might leave it unmatched or pick the wrong season. Fixing it takes seconds:

  1. Open the show and click Fixes near the top of its page, then click Find a match (or Match if it already has one).
  2. Type the show's name to search AniList or TVmaze, or paste a direct link from AniList, MyAnimeList, TVmaze, or IMDb and click Look up.
  3. Click the right result to apply it.

Once you pick a match yourself, Anibeam locks it in and will never overwrite it on future scans.

Fixing several shows at once

Open the Fix matches tab in Library (also reachable from SettingsLibrary via Open Show info). Unmatched shows appear right at the top under Not recognized, and you can step through them one after another without returning to the grid.

Fix misnumbered episodes or extras

Sometimes a release names episodes in an unusual way (like continuous numbering across seasons, or specials mixed in with regular episodes). You don't have to rename the files on disk unless you want to:

  1. Open the show, click Fixes, and choose Files.
  2. Look at the Reads as column to spot any file Anibeam misread. Under Should be, pick what it actually is (an Episode with its real number, or an Opening, Ending, Preview, or Special).
  3. Click Apply (the button shows your count, such as Apply 2 fixes).

Anibeam remembers your file fixes across every future scan. If an airing show's episode count or release dates look incomplete, you can also adjust them under Fixes > Episodes and dates.

Move or remove a folder

Each library folder has its own row in SettingsLibrary:

  • External drive unplugged: The folder shows as Unavailable, and your library keeps its records until the drive comes back. Plug the drive back in and click Rescan.
  • Moved a folder to a new location: Remove the old folder row and click Add folder to add its new path.
  • Removing a folder: Clicking Remove takes that folder's shows and local history out of Anibeam, while leaving all of your actual video files completely untouched on disk.
