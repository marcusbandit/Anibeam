Why connect an anime list?

Connecting AniList or MyAnimeList turns Anibeam into an automatic tracker, so you never have to remember which episode you stopped on.

  • Automatic progress updates: Finish an episode in Anibeam and it is marked watched on your list right away.
  • Two-way sync: Ratings, list status (Watching, Completed, On hold, Planning, Dropped), rewatches, and notes stay in sync between Anibeam and your account.
  • Smarter browsing: See your Watching and Planning shows under Library > On my list, see list badges on Airing, and unlock personalized Recommended for you picks on Home.
No account required

You never have to connect an account to use Anibeam. Without one, Anibeam tracks everything locally on your computer. And when you do connect, automatic playback updates only move your episode count forward unless you intentionally mark episodes unwatched by hand.

Connect AniList

In official Anibeam releases, connecting AniList takes just a few clicks:

  1. Open SettingsAccounts (or the Your list step during first-time setup).
  2. Click Connect AniList.
  3. Your web browser opens AniList's approval page. Sign in to your AniList account if asked, then approve Anibeam.
  4. Return to Anibeam. Your username appears under Your anime lists and your progress syncs automatically.

Because sign-in happens directly on AniList's website in your browser, Anibeam never sees your password.

Built Anibeam from source? Manual AniList setup

Official installers include Anibeam's registered AniList app. If you compiled Anibeam yourself without bundled credentials, Anibeam will show a short setup dialog to register your own free AniList client once:

  1. Click Open the developer page to open AniList's developer settings in your browser, then create a new client with any name.
  2. Back in Anibeam, click the redirect address chip (Copy the address) and paste it into AniList's Redirect URL box exactly as copied, then save the client.
  3. Copy the numeric Client ID from AniList, paste it into Anibeam's Client ID box, and click Connect.

Connect MyAnimeList

Connecting MyAnimeList works the same way in official builds:

  1. Open SettingsAccounts.
  2. Click Connect MyAnimeList.
  3. Sign in to MyAnimeList in the browser tab that opens and approve Anibeam.
  4. Return to Anibeam once the browser confirms you are connected.
Built Anibeam from source? Manual MyAnimeList setup

If you built Anibeam from source without bundled credentials, Anibeam walks you through creating your own MyAnimeList API ID once:

  1. Click Open the developer page to open MyAnimeList's API config page and create a new ID with App Type set to other.
  2. Copy the redirect address from Anibeam and paste it into App Redirect URL on MyAnimeList exactly as copied, fill in the remaining required fields, and submit.
  3. Copy the long Client ID into Anibeam's Client ID box. Leave Client secret empty unless you registered a web app type.
  4. Click Connect and approve Anibeam in your browser.

Using both at once

You can connect both AniList and MyAnimeList at the same time. Whenever you finish an episode, rate a show, change a status, or save notes, Anibeam updates both accounts.

Use Main list under SettingsAccounts to choose which service's progress numbers appear on your library cards.

Everyday tracking settings

Once at least one account is connected, a few helpful controls appear in SettingsAccounts:

  • Sync now: Pulls the latest progress from your connected lists immediately. Anibeam also refreshes automatically each time you open the app.
  • Move to On hold: Automatically moves a Watching show to On hold if you haven't played an episode for a while (60 days by default; adjust with the slider). As soon as you watch another episode, Anibeam moves it right back to Watching.
  • Lists on this computer: If you rated shows or set list statuses before signing in, click Review and move to preview and upload those local entries to your AniList or MyAnimeList account.
  • Pause syncing for one show: Click Hide on any show's page to hide it from your library and stop syncing it to your online lists.
  • Disconnect: Click Disconnect next to an account to remove its sign-in from this computer. Your online list is not affected.
