What you need to download episodes

Downloading inside Anibeam uses two pieces working together:

  • At least one download source: Tells Anibeam where to search for episodes. See Download sources if you haven't added a source list or feed yet.
  • A torrent client: The background engine that downloads the episode files. Anibeam already includes a built-in qBittorrent client, so most people don't need to install or configure anything extra.
Already have a magnet link?

You can paste any magnet: link straight into the search box on the Downloads page (or click magnet links in your browser) to download straight into the right show folder. See Paste or open a magnet link below.

Built-in client vs. your own qBittorrent

You can choose how downloads run under SettingsDownloads > Torrent client > Which client:

Easiest

Built-in

Anibeam runs its own private qBittorrent quietly in the background. Nothing to install, no passwords to type, and episodes save straight into your library folder.

It keeps running after you close Anibeam so subscribed shows still finish downloading.

My own

Connects to a qBittorrent you already manage yourself, whether it runs on this computer, a home server, or a remote seedbox.

You can save several clients and switch between them right from the Downloads page.

There is also an Auto option: it connects to your own qBittorrent if one is already answering at the configured address, and seamlessly falls back to the built-in client otherwise.

Connect your own qBittorrent

If you already use qBittorrent and want Anibeam to send downloads there instead of using the built-in client:

  1. Open qBittorrent and go to ToolsOptionsWeb UI.
  2. Check Web User Interface (Remote control), note the port (usually 8080), and set a username and password.
  3. In Anibeam, open SettingsDownloads and set Which client to My own.
  4. Enter the Address (for example, http://localhost:8080), along with your Username and Password.
  5. Click Save and test. When the connection works, Anibeam displays the qBittorrent version that answered.

Your password is stored in your system's password manager. If you switch between multiple setups (such as a local client and a remote server), use Saved clients to store more than one and switch between them from the client chip at the top of the Downloads page.

Where downloaded episodes are saved

With the built-in client: Anibeam automatically saves episodes straight into your library folder, putting each show in its own subfolder (or into the show's existing folder if it is already in your library) and movies into a Movies folder. As soon as an episode finishes, it pops into your library on its own.

Want to save new downloads somewhere else?

  1. Open SettingsDownloads and look at the Save to row.
  2. Click Choose folder and pick your preferred folder. New downloads use it right away.
  3. Click Reset anytime to go back to your library folder.
Using your own qBittorrent?

Your own qBittorrent saves files wherever its own settings tell it to. Make sure that download folder is added under SettingsLibrary (or is inside a folder already listed there), otherwise finished episodes will never show up in your Anibeam library.

Download episodes of a show

There are three ways to grab episodes depending on how you like to browse:

Option 1: From any show's page (recommended)

  1. Open any show from Library, Airing, or Home recommendations and click Get episodes.
  2. Anibeam searches your sources and groups results cleanly by release group and episode cubes. Pick a row or individual episodes you want. Episodes you already have on disk are automatically skipped unless you turn on Replace current episodes.
  3. Click the download button (such as Get 12 episodes).
Watch while the rest downloads

Turn on Download in order (or Download in episode order) so Episode 1 finishes first instead of downloading random pieces across the whole season.

Option 2: Search directly on the Downloads page

Open Downloads and type into the search box at the top. Click a show from the suggestions to open the episode grid, or press Enter to search raw release names across your enabled sources. From the release table, filter by release group, quality, or keyword (prefix a word with - to exclude it), check the releases you want, and click Download.

Option 3: Paste a magnet link

If you already copied a magnet: link from your browser, paste it directly into the search box on the Downloads page instead of searching. See Paste or open a magnet link right below.

Paste or open a magnet link

Already have a torrent magnet link from your browser? You do not need to open a separate torrent app or sort the files by hand:

  1. Copy any magnet: link from your browser.
  2. Open the Downloads page and paste it straight into the search box at the top.
  3. Anibeam reads the torrent name and offers matching shows from your library and AniList, plus Download as a film at the bottom.
  4. Click the right match (or press Enter) to download it directly into that show's folder (or your Movies folder).
One-click magnet links from your browser (Linux)

Under SettingsDownloads > Magnet links, click Open them in Anibeam. From then on, clicking any magnet link in your browser jumps straight into Anibeam's Downloads picker (this even works before you add any download sources).

Pasted magnets always download the whole torrent you picked (Rolling downloads is skipped for them). If a magnet link turns out to contain something other than video files, Anibeam lets you know and moves the download out to your regular system Downloads folder so it does not clutter your anime library.

Follow a show as it airs

For seasonal anime that release a new episode every week, you can subscribe once and let Anibeam grab every new episode automatically:

  1. Open Get episodes on an airing show, pick your preferred release group, and turn on Subscribe for new episodes (or click Subscribe after narrowing a search on the Downloads page).
  2. Each week when a matching episode comes out, Anibeam downloads it straight into that show's folder.

View and manage all of your active subscriptions under the Following tab on the Downloads page, and click Unsubscribe anytime to stop following a show. When Unsubscribe from finished shows is enabled in SettingsDownloads, Anibeam automatically tidies up a subscription 16 days after the season's finale airs.

Save disk space with Rolling downloads

Downloading a 100-episode or 500-episode anime all at once can easily eat up hundreds of gigabytes. Rolling downloads solves that by keeping just a small buffer of upcoming episodes on disk:

  • Instead of downloading the whole show upfront, Anibeam downloads a set number of episodes ahead of where you are (12 episodes by default).
  • Each time you finish watching an episode, Anibeam automatically starts downloading the next one in line.
  • If you turn on automatic cleanup, older watched episodes are moved to your trash once you move past them.

Configure your defaults under SettingsDownloads > Rolling downloads: toggle Auto-enable for long shows, choose how many Episodes ahead to keep ready, pick how long a show has to be before rolling kicks in, and choose whether watched episodes are deleted or kept.

Other ways to tidy up disk space

  • Gaps in your library (on the Downloads page): Shows any aired episodes missing from shows you already have, so you can fill in gaps with a click.
  • Downloaded (on the Downloads page): Lists every show on disk sorted biggest first, with a Delete watched menu to clear watched episodes or completely watched finished shows in one go. Everything deleted through Anibeam goes to your system trash so you can recover it if you change your mind.
