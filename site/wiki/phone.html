Install Anibeam on Android or iPhone

Anibeam has a standalone mobile app for both Android and iPhone. Download links live on the download page.

Android (Android 8+)

Download the .apk file from the download page and tap it to install. Android will ask once to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.

iPhone (iOS 17+)

Anibeam for iPhone is distributed as an unsigned IPA for sideloading. Add the Anibeam source to AltStore, SideStore, or LiveContainer from the download page so your sideloading app signs it and keeps it updated.

Two ways to use the phone app

You can use the phone app completely on its own, pair it with your desktop library, or do both at the same time:

Standalone on your phone

Keep your own library right on the phone, search sources, download episodes with the built-in torrent engine, watch offline with the built-in mpv player, and sync directly to AniList or MyAnimeList.

Stream from your computer

Connect securely to your desktop Anibeam over Tailscale to browse your computer's full library from anywhere and stream episodes to your phone with automatic quality adjustment.

Stream your computer's library over Tailscale

Tailscale is a free app that creates a private, encrypted network between your own devices. Using it means you never have to mess with router port forwarding or expose your home computer to the public internet: only devices signed into your Tailscale network can reach Anibeam.

  1. Install Tailscale on both your computer and your phone, sign in with the same account, and make sure Tailscale is turned on.
  2. On your computer, open SettingsAccounts and scroll down to Phone app.
  3. Turn on Share over Tailscale (and make sure Stream episodes is turned on). One or more addresses will appear underneath (for example, my-computer:8790 or 100.64.0.1:8790).
  4. On your phone, open MoreYour computer (or the Your computer step during first-time setup), type or paste that address, tap Test, and tap Save.
  5. Open the Library tab on your phone and use the switcher at the top to flip between This phone and your computer's library.
Stream even when the desktop window is closed

Normally your computer shares its library while Anibeam is open. To keep sharing in the background with no window open, run anibeam serve on your computer, or on Linux enable the background service listed under Host service in SettingsAccounts:

$systemctl --user enable --now anibeam-host

Sharing with family or multiple devices

Under SettingsAccounts > Devices on your tailnet, you can see every device on your Tailscale network and toggle access on or off for any device. If you share your tailnet with another person, Anibeam keeps each person's watch progress separate on your computer.

Stream quality and mobile data

Under MoreStreaming on your phone, choose how episodes streamed from your computer play:

  • Auto (recommended): Plays the full-quality original file on Wi-Fi, and automatically converts to 720p on mobile data or when Tailscale routes through a slower relay.
  • Original: Always streams the untouched video file (best on fast Wi-Fi).
  • Converted (1080p, 720p, or 480p): Has your computer convert the video on the fly to save mobile data.

Downloading directly on your phone

If you download episodes straight to your phone for offline viewing (like flights or commutes), Anibeam includes built-in safeguards so anime files don't quietly drain your data plan or fill up your storage:

  • Wi-Fi only: Turned on by default so downloads wait until you are on Wi-Fi (a single anime episode can be 1 to 2 GB). You can allow mobile data under More > Network.
  • Storage cap: Set a limit in GB (for example, 18 GB holds roughly 12 episodes) under More > Storage. Anibeam pauses new downloads once you hit the cap until you delete watched episodes.
  • Rolling downloads: Works just like desktop for long shows, keeping only a handful of upcoming episodes on your phone.
  • Download sources: Add your source list or custom feeds under More. The phone app uses its own built-in torrent engine and stops seeding as soon as an episode finishes to save battery and data.
